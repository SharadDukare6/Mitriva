Child Safety Standards

Published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

Community Guidelines · Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Mitriva is only for adults aged 18 and older. Minors may not register, create accounts, appear in dating profiles, or use Mitriva's matchmaking and social discovery services.

Zero tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

Mitriva expressly prohibits any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, coercion, trafficking, sexualization of children, and any child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Users may not create, upload, request, distribute, store, promote, link to, or facilitate such material or behavior through profiles, photographs, messages, or any other feature.

Preventing and reporting violations

Users must provide truthful age information and follow Mitriva's adult-only rules. Users can report or block other profiles from within the app. Reports involving suspected CSAE or CSAM can also be submitted directly to mitriva@iconaisconsulting.com. Do not email or forward CSAM itself; share account identifiers, message details, and other non-explicit information that enables investigation.

We review reports, may remove prohibited content, restrict or terminate accounts, and preserve relevant evidence where legally permitted. We escalate confirmed or reasonably suspected unlawful child-abuse content to the appropriate Indian or other competent regional/national authority in accordance with applicable law. We cooperate with legitimate requests from law enforcement. For immediate risk to a child, contact local emergency services or law enforcement without delay.

Safety process and legal compliance

ICONAIS Consulting Private Limited maintains an escalation procedure to receive and triage child-safety reports, restrict access to implicated content and accounts, preserve necessary evidence, and report confirmed CSAM to the appropriate competent authorities where legally required. The process applies to reports received through the in-app reporting function and the designated safety email.
